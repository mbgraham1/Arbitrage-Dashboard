---
name: Execution-lock heartbeat coverage
description: Lock heartbeats are OWNERSHIP-SCOPED (AsyncLocalStorage); only the lock holder's private calls beat; stale window sized to one HTTP timeout.
---
Rule: any awaited private exchange call (Kraken AND Coinbase — placement, cancel, unwind, fee/status lookups) made by the LOCK-HOLDING execution must beat the execution-lock heartbeat on start, finish, every ≤5s during rate-limit backoff, and every 5s while queued in the per-key serial limiter. The heartbeat is ownership-scoped: the executor binds `bindLockHeartbeat(liveLockHeartbeat(gen))` right after acquiring the lock (AsyncLocalStorage scope in the exchange lib); the wrapper checks `liveLockOwned(gen)` so it self-neutralizes on KILL/eviction/release. Calls OUTSIDE the owner's scope beat NOTHING — a process-global heartbeat was rejected in review because unrelated traffic (dashboard reads, other accounts' backed-up calls) could keep a dead lock alive and defeat FORCE stale-lock eviction.

**Why:** Lock staleness eviction is silence-based; a call under rate-limit backoff or stuck queued behind other paced calls used to go quiet >15s, letting FORCE eviction (15s) kill a healthy resting leg. With scoped per-call + queue beats the worst legitimate silence is one HTTP timeout (~10s), so the window is 30s (FORCE 15s). Evicting a live holder mid-order lets two executions double-spend the same balance.

**How to apply:** every new executor that acquires the live lock must call `bindLockHeartbeat(liveLockHeartbeat(lockGen))` immediately after acquisition. New venues/private-call helpers route through the beating wrapper (or beat() around the fetch) — beats only fire inside the owner's scope. Never tighten LIVE_LOCK_STALE_MS below ~3× the longest un-beaten await. Gotcha: the limiter captures the scoped heartbeat ONCE at call entry (fake timers and setInterval don't reliably propagate ALS context).

**Cooperative KILL + unwind:** the liveLockOwned checks between legs must UNWIND held inventory before throwing — an abort message claiming "unwound" without an actual market unwind strands inventory. Tests can fake only Date (vi.useFakeTimers({toFake:["Date"]})) to advance heartbeat silence past eviction thresholds while real timers keep executor poll loops alive.
