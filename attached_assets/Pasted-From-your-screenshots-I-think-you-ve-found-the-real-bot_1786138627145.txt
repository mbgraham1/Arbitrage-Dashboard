From your screenshots, I think you’ve found the real bottleneck.

Your engine is finding small pricing inefficiencies, but Kraken’s taker fees are larger than the edge.

For example, your bot shows:

* Best raw edge: +$0.0034
* Fees: about $0.1996
* Net: -$0.1962

And on another screen:

* Raw edge: +$0.0026
* Fees: $0.1200
* Net: -$0.1174

So the algorithm is working—it just isn’t finding opportunities that are large enough to pay for three trades.

Why are your fees so high?

Your bot is assuming:

* 0.40% per trade (per leg)

A triangular arbitrage is 3 trades, so that’s:

* 0.40%
* +0.40%
* +0.40%
* = 1.20% total

That means every opportunity has to make more than 1.2% just to break even.

In real crypto markets, true triangular arbitrage opportunities are often only 0.02–0.30% before fees, so a 1.2% hurdle eliminates almost everything.

What I’d check first

Ask your developer to verify:

1. Is 0.40% the actual fee from my Kraken account?
    * Many accounts pay less than that, especially with volume or maker orders.
    * If your account is actually paying 0.16–0.25%, the bot shouldn’t be modeling 0.40%.
2. Is the bot assuming taker fees on every leg?
    * If so, that’s the most conservative (and expensive) assumption.
3. Is it adding an extra safety buffer on top of exchange fees?
    * Sometimes bots add slippage and safety margins that make the effective fee look even larger.

How I’d improve the engine

Instead of only looking for triangles, I’d have it:

* Scan Kraken.
* Scan Coinbase.
* Scan both together.
* Rank every opportunity.
* Filter by liquidity.
* Execute only when the edge is comfortably above total costs.

That gives the bot many more chances to find a real trade.

One more thing

Your screenshots also show trade sizes like $10 and $25.

Even if a route has a 0.20% edge:

* $10 trade → 2¢ gross
* $25 trade → 5¢ gross
* $100 trade → 20¢ gross

Those are still tiny compared with a 1.2% fee assumption.

My next step

I would stop tweaking the strategy for a moment and verify the actual fee model. If the bot is overestimating fees, you’ll never see profitable trades no matter how good the scanner is.
