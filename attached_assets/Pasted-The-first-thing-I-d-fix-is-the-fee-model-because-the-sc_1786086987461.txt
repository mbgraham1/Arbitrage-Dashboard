The first thing I’d fix is the fee model, because the screenshot is basically screaming that the bot is subtracting about 1.2% from every route.

That pattern is too consistent:

-1.203%
-1.207%
-1.212%
-1.214%
-1.216%

That looks very much like:

~0.40% Kraken fee × 3 trades = ~1.20%

So the scanner may actually be doing the math correctly — but it also means 3-leg triangular arbitrage with taker orders is almost impossible at that fee tier.

What I would change

First, make the analyzer separate these numbers instead of hiding everything inside “net”:

RAW TRIANGLE EDGE      +0.034%
LEG 1 FEE              -0.400%
LEG 2 FEE              -0.400%
LEG 3 FEE              -0.400%
ORDER-BOOK SLIPPAGE    -0.018%
--------------------------------
FINAL NET              -1.184%

That immediately tells you whether you’re fighting the market or a coding bug.

Then I’d verify the fee calculation is structured like this:

amount = starting_usd

# leg 1
amount_after_leg1 = leg1_output * (Decimal("1") - fee_rate)

# leg 2
amount_after_leg2 = leg2_output * (Decimal("1") - fee_rate)

# leg 3
final_usd = leg3_output * (Decimal("1") - fee_rate)

profit = final_usd - starting_usd
profit_pct = profit / starting_usd * Decimal("100")

Not this:

profit_pct = raw_profit_pct - combined_fee

unless combined_fee is explicitly the correct total for all three legs.

Also make sure you’re not doing both:

price_with_fee = ...

and later:

profit -= fees

because that would double-count the fees.

The bigger issue

If your actual Kraken taker fee really is around 0.40% per leg, the bot needs roughly:

1.20%+ raw triangular mispricing

just to break even before spread/slippage.

That’s enormous for BTC/ETH/XRP-type liquid markets. You can make the scanner technically perfect and it still won’t produce many profitable taker triangles.

So I’d build CAT_ARB around two modes:
TAKER MODE
Fast execution
Three taker fees
Requires a very large edge
MAKER MODE 
Lower fees
Much smaller required edge
But orders may not fill and the triangle can disappear

And I’d have the dashboard automatically use your actual Kraken fee tier, rather than a guessed fee.

One other thing I’d add immediately

Put a diagnostic box above your routes:

KRAKEN FEE DIAGNOSTIC

Fee per leg:       0.400%
3-leg fee drag:    1.195%
Avg spread drag:   0.014%
Break-even edge:   1.209%

Best raw edge now: 0.027%

RESULT:
No executable triangle

Then you’ll instantly know why everything is red.

So I would not try to “fix” this by lowering the fee number. I would first confirm whether that ~1.2% is genuinely three Kraken fees. If it is, then the real problem is the economics of using three taker orders—not your scanner.
